The rule

In a CommunityUse EnFin. It captures the Energy Community bonus.
Not in oneUse LightReach, the default TPO partner everywhere else.
This is a ZIP-level screen built from three DOE/EPA layers: the fossil-fuel-employment county designation, coal-closure census tracts, and EPA-listed brownfield sites. It answers fast, but it isn't a substitute for the address-exact determination. Some ZIPs only partially overlap a qualifying area (flagged above); verify those on the Baker Tilly map before you submit. Status can also change on the annual federal redetermination. Not for substantiating a tax return position, this is directional analysis, not tax advice.
