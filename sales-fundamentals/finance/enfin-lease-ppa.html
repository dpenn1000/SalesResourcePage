Selling With EnFin

EnFin is Q Cells' financing arm, and it carries the lowest credit bar of Trinity's TPO partners. One rule decides when to use it: check the property against the Energy Community map before you knock. In a Community, EnFin. Not in one, LightReach.

Who Gets EnFin

One rule decides the partner, and it runs before you ever open a proposal: check the property's ZIP against the Energy Community Checker. In a DOE-designated Energy Community, use EnFin. Not in one, use LightReach.

Check before you knock

Type the ZIP into Trinity's Energy Community Checker for an instant read, right from your phone. On a partial-match ZIP, or for a definitive address-exact answer, confirm on the federal Baker Tilly map.

In a Community, EnFin

EnFin captures the Energy Community bonus. That bonus is the reason this partner is in the rotation at all.

Not in one, LightReach

LightReach stays the default TPO partner everywhere the property doesn't qualify.

Your lane: check the ZIP, pick the partner, sell the deal. EnFin's underwriting rules are below. If a property doesn't fit EnFin, LightReach is the fallback partner, not a workaround.
Pull up tonight's appointments. Run each ZIP through the Energy Community Checker live. How many sit in an Energy Community?

AnswerTrinity's Energy Community Checker (ZIP lookup) for a fast read, or the federal Baker Tilly map for an address-exact answer. If the address isn't in a Community, use LightReach instead.

Credit and Approval

620 minimum FICO, the lowest of Trinity's four TPO partners. The pull starts soft and only turns hard once the deal is real.

620 FICO, the floor

Lowest minimum of GoodLeap, IGS, LightReach, and EnFin. If a customer is on the edge everywhere else, check EnFin first.

Soft, then hard

Soft pull at credit approval, converting to a hard pull at NTP Funding (Milestone 1). That's a conversion of the same inquiry, not a new one. Tell the customer up front.

One person, one file

If the co-applicant passes credit, the main customer doesn't have to. Only one person needs to be on title.

  1. Bankruptcy

    3-year look back. Clock starts when they emerge, not at the filing date.

  2. Foreclosure

    4-year look back.

  3. Freeze unlocks

    Experian and TransUnion need to be unfrozen. EnFin doesn't pull Equifax.

  4. ITIN customers

    Accepted. Have a green card or proof of permanent residency ready.

Borderline FICO: wait about 30 days before rerunning, and create a brand new lead each time. Running it again immediately doesn't help and burns an inquiry for nothing.
A customer's FICO comes back at 615. Walk through what you tell them and what you do next.
A customer's FICO comes back at 615. What do you do?

AnswerWait about 30 days before rerunning, and create a brand new lead each time you rerun. 620 is the floor; rerunning immediately doesn't move the number.

Property and Ownership

EnFin covers more property types than most TPO partners, but ownership structure still trips deals. Check both before you build the proposal.

Approved

  • Single family, vacation homes.
  • Duplex / triplex, if the customer owns the whole property.
  • Condos, with HOA approval, a separate meter, and the unit owning its own roof.
  • Manufactured and modular homes, with a HUD plate and a permanent foundation.
  • Apartments, up to 4 units.

Not approved

  • Rental or investment property.
  • Mobile homes.
  • Mixed commercial / residential.
  • Ground mounts, under any circumstances.

Trusts, both kinds

Revocable and irrevocable both work. List the trustee on the contract, not the trust itself. A Certificate of Trust is required.

LLCs and corps: no

Not accepted. EnFin only signs individuals, unlike GoodLeap and LightReach.

Power of attorney: no

Not accepted. A reverse mortgage on the property is fine; a POA arrangement isn't.

Multiple owners

Unlimited people can be on title. If more than one is on the contract, everyone on it has to pass credit, and at least one has to be on title.

The home's in an LLC. Role-play how you'd find that out at the door, and what you'd tell the customer.
The home's in an LLC. Can EnFin fund it?

AnswerNo. EnFin doesn't accept LLCs or corporations as applicants. Move the deal to a partner that does, or restructure it into personal names.

Contract and Terms

Lease in NY, MD, and PA. PPA in CT, NJ, and MA. The guarantee and the true-up schedule are the same everywhere EnFin operates.

90% production guarantee

Starts Year 2, checked every year after. Tolerance runs -5% to +10% on the production calculations.

True-up every year

Starting Year 2, then annually. Other partners true up every 2 or 3 years; EnFin checks every year.

No cap on contract value

Capped by max system size instead. The project still has to pass Year 1 savings and max PPW for the state.

Buy-out after Year 5

On home sale, or at the end of the contract term.

English only. No translators at any point in the process, and no exceptions once the deal is signed.
Walk through the true-up schedule out loud, like you'd explain it at the table.
When does the production guarantee start, and what does it cover?

AnswerYear 2, and every year after: 90% of projected production. The production calculation itself carries a -5% to +10% tolerance.

Utility, Signing, and Getting Paid

The utility bill and the validation call are where EnFin deals stall. Both have one specific, avoidable failure mode.

One late bill kills it

$100 or more past due, reported to a bureau in the last 12 months, is an automatic decline. Even an unreported past-due balance can flag underwriting.

Residential meters only

GLP meters aren't accepted. Most major utilities work; municipal utilities (Munis) don't.

The validation call is customer-only

They get a link after signing. You can be in the room, but you can't help them answer, verbally or otherwise.

First payment: 30 days post-PTO

Timed so the customer never pays for a system that isn't producing yet.

Payment options: ACH earns a $10 discount. Checks, debit cards, portal payments, and phone payments all work too. No credit cards.
Role-play the validation-call warning. How do you tell a customer you can't help them answer without it sounding like you're hiding something?
What single utility-bill issue causes an automatic decline?

Answer$100 or more past due on the electric bill, reported to a credit bureau within the last 12 months.
